How Hoist works

  • Each day brings a short session: the flags due for review, plus new ones (10 a day unless you change it in Settings). A new day starts at 4am.
  • A new flag starts as multiple choice. Get it right twice in a row and you’ll type its name instead.
  • Flags you know come back less often each time: after a day, then 3 days, then about 2 weeks, and so on.
  • If you miss a flag, you’ll see it again a few cards later and again tomorrow, back at multiple choice. Picking the right answer after “Show choices” counts as a miss too, but the flag stays at typing.
  • Slow answers and typos still count as right, but the flag comes back sooner.
  • A flag is mastered once you’re typing its name and Hoist expects you’ll still remember it in 3 weeks. If you play every day, your first mastered flags will show up after about 3 weeks.
  • Once you’re done for the day, Learn 5 more flags adds new flags. Extra practice goes over flags you’ve already seen, always including today’s misses, and doesn’t change your schedule.
